🏅 Mission Complete

Every decision you made is connected to a real-world outcome. Here's what your choices meant — and what you learned.

✅
Created a unique username (not real name)
Prevented direct identity linking across platforms
Real world: Usernames with real names can be cross-referenced to find full profiles on other sites.
⚠️
Used Pixel's full real name in the Name field
Combined with DOB → Identity confidence reached 91%
Real world: Full name + age is enough for data brokers to build a complete record on someone.
🔄
Left default tracking toggles ON
Behavioral profiling began within 24 hours of signup
Real world: Defaults are intentionally set to benefit companies, not users. Always check them.
💡
Used "Ohio" instead of full city/ZIP
Location exposure stayed low — data kept broad
Real world: Broad location (state) is 10x less identifying than a ZIP code. Use the minimum necessary.
🚫
Left phone number field blank
Prevented phone-based spam and phishing attempts
Real world: Phone numbers are the #1 way personal data is sold to third-party marketers.
The Big Takeaway: "Just because I have access to information doesn't mean I should use it — and even small decisions can combine to reveal much more than I expect."
